I am a physical oceanographer studying how ocean circulation shapes transport, ecosystem structure, and biological hotspots. My research uses observations, satellite data, high-frequency radar, numerical models, and Lagrangian diagnostics to understand ocean dynamics in coastal and polar systems.

Field Experience

My field experience includes Arctic fjord work aboard the RV Marie Tharp, early-career chief scientist training aboard the USCGC Healy, Antarctic field seasons at Palmer Station, glider operations, high-frequency radar deployments, CTD casts, plankton sampling, and small-boat surveys.
