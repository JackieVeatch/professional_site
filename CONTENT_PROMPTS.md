# Website content prompts

The first version is complete using information already in the August 2026 CV. These prompts would help make a future revision more personal and specific. Short, informal answers are perfect—I can shape them into polished website copy.

## Research story

1. What is the one ocean question you want people to associate with your name?
2. What first made you curious about the way currents organize marine life?
3. In plain language, what could scientists or decision-makers do better if your current postdoctoral research succeeds?

## Research projects

For each project you want featured, please answer:

- What was the central question?
- What did you personally lead or build?
- What is the most surprising result?
- Why does the result matter beyond physical oceanography?
- Is there a paper, dataset, code repository, talk, or media story to link?
- Which image best represents the project, and who should receive photo credit?

## Teaching and mentoring

1. Tell me about one moment when a student changed how you think about teaching.
2. Which course or workshop are you proudest of designing, and what made it work?
3. What should a student be able to do—or believe about themselves—after learning with you?
4. May the site mention or quote any student feedback? If so, paste one or two short examples and note whether attribution is permitted.

## Fieldwork

1. Which field campaign best captures how you work on a team?
2. Describe one difficult field decision or troubleshooting moment and what you learned from it.
3. Do you have 4–8 field photos that can be published, with location, year, photographer, and a one-sentence caption for each?

## Public impact and future direction

1. Which audiences do you most want to reach outside academia?
2. Which policy, outreach, or professional-service contribution feels most meaningful to you, and why?
3. What kinds of collaborations or roles are you interested in next?

## Practical details

- Preferred public name: Jacquelyn, Jackie, or both?
- Preferred pronouns, if you want them shown.
- A LinkedIn URL, if you want it included.
- Your preferred location wording (for example, “Woods Hole, MA” or “Cape Cod, MA”).
- Whether you want the site to advertise speaking, mentoring, consulting, media, or job opportunities.
- Any funders, institutions, collaborators, or photographers that require acknowledgment.
